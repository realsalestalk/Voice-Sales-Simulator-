02 — How it works

Three tiers, one site.

Every site ships in three rendering modes and picks one per visitor. A gaming desktop gets the full aurora. A mid-range laptop gets a lighter version. A phone on a metered connection gets a fast static page that still looks deliberate — because that visitor is the one most likely to call.

  1. Tier 3 Full experience. Real-time aurora, five-octave noise, pointer parallax, post-processing.
  2. Tier 2 Standard. Same shader, reduced detail, capped pixel ratio, no post FX.
  3. Tier 1 Static. No WebGL downloaded at all. Designed gradient, full content, fastest possible load.
